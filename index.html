A practical process

How a project takes shape.

I test a small first version with real files, messages and tasks before improving it.

01 — scope

Find the bottleneck

Map the hand-off, repetition or decision that is slowing work down.

02 — build

Make a usable first version

Create a focused prototype or page that demonstrates the core job.

03 — test

Use real scenarios

Try it with representative files, messages and tasks rather than idealised examples.

04 — improve

Keep what works

Refine the parts that prove useful: reliability, flow, copy, handover or next integration.

Scope comes first.

Automation is not always the answer. The right outcome may be a clearer process, a simpler website, a spreadsheet improvement, or deciding not to automate yet.
